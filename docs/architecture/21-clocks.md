# 21: Clocks

Every timer and deadline in the tree reads the clock of its class, and the classes A to H below are the letters code comments cite. The chapter states the two tolerances the wall clock may carry between machines and works the case of a device waking after a week against them. The away gate that counts time away is owned by [14-at-rest-encryption.md](14-at-rest-encryption.md), certificate expiry by [19-device-to-device.md](19-device-to-device.md), the replica's grace countdown by [15-replica-retention.md](15-replica-retention.md#local-eviction), and the case of several servers sharing one database by [10-subscription-materializer.md](10-subscription-materializer.md).

## What the clocks do across a suspend

| Clock | Counts suspend | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `std::time::Instant` and `tokio::time` on Linux and Android | no, `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` | [Rust `Instant`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/time/struct.Instant.html#underlying-system-calls), [clock_gettime(2)](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/clock_gettime.2.html) |
| `std::time::Instant` and `tokio::time` on Apple | no, `CLOCK_UPTIME_RAW` | [Rust `Instant`](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/time/struct.Instant.html#underlying-system-calls), [`mach_absolute_time`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/kernel/1462446-mach_absolute_time) |
| `std::time::Instant` and `tokio::time` on Windows | yes, `QueryPerformanceCounter` | [Acquiring high-resolution time stamps](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/sysinfo/acquiring-high-resolution-time-stamps) |
| `CLOCK_BOOTTIME`, Darwin `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`, Android `elapsedRealtime` | yes | [clock_gettime(2)](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/clock_gettime.2.html), [`mach_continuous_time`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/kernel/1646199-mach_continuous_time), [SystemClock](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/os/SystemClock) |
| `performance.now()` | unspecified, browsers differ | [HR-Time](https://w3c.github.io/hr-time/), [w3c/hr-time#115](https://github.com/w3c/hr-time/issues/115) |
| Wall clocks, `SystemTime`, `Date.now()`, Postgres `now()` | yes, and they step either way | [clock_gettime(2)](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/clock_gettime.2.html), [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/functions-datetime.html#FUNCTIONS-DATETIME-CURRENT) |

Rust leaves open whether `Instant` counts suspend ([rust-lang/rust#87906](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/87906)), so no rule below depends on it.

## The rule per class

| Class | Clock | Across a suspend |
|---|---|---|
| A bound on one operation in progress (connect, handshake, hello, write and drain bounds, `EXCHANGE_BOUND`, `CALL_BOUND`, hotspot join and scan bounds, `SessionConfig`'s first-frame, resume and write-retry budgets, the keyring prompt and unlock windows, the demos' login window) | process monotonic, `tokio::time` | resumes with its remainder, or fires at wake on Windows. A network bound is a minute or less and its socket or radio does not outlive a long sleep. A prompt or login window waits on a person, who is away for the sleep too, so both behaviors end the operation correctly |
| A pace or retry wait (`RetryPolicy` backoff, the client's machine-loop ticks, discovery redial, the peer link's ping and silence limit, the status-list refetch, the CDC reconnect) | process monotonic | resumes with its remainder. `RetryPolicy`'s jitter spreads episodes that start together |
| A periodic server sweep (the writer sweep, the content sweep, the slot lag watch) | `tokio::time::interval` with `MissedTickBehavior::Delay` | one pass after the stall, never a burst of passes |
| A deadline inside the browser worker (hello, staged, resolve and resend waits in `connetto-web`) | `performance.now()` | a wall step cannot lengthen or shorten it. `Date.now()` stays for identities and user-visible times |
| Time away and the writer-answer deadline | sleep-counting monotonic (`CLOCK_BOOTTIME`, Darwin `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`, `QueryPerformanceCounter`, `performance.now()` in the browser). Time away also reads the wall clock, the larger delta winning ([14-at-rest-encryption.md](14-at-rest-encryption.md)) | counts the sleep. Time away in a browser whose `performance.now()` stops in sleep is covered by the wall delta, and the writer-answer deadline there resumes after wake |
| A countdown in the replica (watch grace in `subscriptions.rs`, aggregate `updated_at`) | the replica's wall clock, `now_secs` in [clock.rs](../../crates/connetto-client/src/clock.rs) | keeps running through suspend and offline, as [15-replica-retention.md](15-replica-retention.md#local-eviction) decides. A stop moment ahead of the current time, a wall clock stepped back, is re-anchored to now under the write exemption, so the countdown never outlasts its grace and the write never rides an upload |
| A wall deadline written on one machine and checked on another (certificate windows, revocation lists, a registration code's expiry the minting device writes, access, resume and share-key tokens, content tickets) | wall, with [`connetto_core::clock::DEVICE_SKEW`] (5 min) wherever a device or peer clock is on either side, and [`connetto_core::clock::SERVER_SKEW`] (60 s) when deployment servers both sign and check | not affected. A revoked serial stays on the published list until its certificate's expiry plus `DEVICE_SKEW`, since a peer accepts a certificate that long past `not_after` |
| A deadline Postgres stores (session idle and absolute deadlines, bans, retained provider tokens, a registration code's `used_at`, certificate liveness, file timestamps and the sweep grace) | Postgres `now()`, in the statement that writes or reads it. An in-memory store serves one process and keeps the same deadlines on the process clock | not affected. Several servers sharing one database ([10-subscription-materializer.md](10-subscription-materializer.md)) agree on one clock, the rule stated for `auth_events` in [audit.rs](../../crates/connetto-server/src/audit.rs) |

RFC 7519 allows "small leeway, usually no more than a few minutes" ([4.1.4](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7519#section-4.1.4)) when a wall deadline written on one machine is checked on another. Five minutes serves devices, whose clocks drift off-grid ([19-device-to-device.md](19-device-to-device.md)). Servers run NTP, and a wider leeway there only keeps a stolen access token or ticket usable longer. One five-minute tolerance everywhere was rejected for that reason.

## A device waking after a week

Watches still held when the device slept start no countdown. Those dropped in the grace before sleep are past it on wake. Eviction waits for the transport ([15-replica-retention.md](15-replica-retention.md#local-eviction)), and the first connected pump step, `drain_dropped` in [live.rs](../../crates/connetto-client/src/live.rs), unsubscribes each expired record in turn, evicting rows scoped to its own tables. Operation bounds and pace waits resume with their remainder, or fire at wake on Windows, each within its own bound and jitter. The away gate counts the week and asks the prompt if the gate is set ([14-at-rest-encryption.md](14-at-rest-encryption.md)). A certificate that expired during the week stops the peer link and raises `ClientEvent::CertificateExpired` ([19-device-to-device.md](19-device-to-device.md)).
